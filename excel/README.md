## 1. TCS Financial Intelligence Dashboard (FY2023 - FY2026)

Analysis of the consolidated financials of Tata Consultancy Services (₹ crore): revenue, profit, margins, balance sheet and cash flow.

- KPI cards: Revenue, Net Profit, Operating Margin, Revenue CAGR
- Charts: Revenue and Net Profit trend, Operating Margin trend, Assets vs Liabilities, Operating Cash Flow vs Investing
- Key insights: steady operating margin of about 27%, strong cash generation, minimal debt
- Data source: [TCS annual reports / other source]

## 2. Vrinda Store Annual Sales Dashboard (2026)

An interactive dashboard analysing a year of sales and orders, built with pivot tables, charts and slicers.

- Monthly sales vs number of orders
- Sales split by gender (Women 64%, Men 36%)
- Order status: Delivered 92%, Cancelled 3%, Returned 3%, Refunded 2%
- Top 5 states by sales
- Order channels: Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio and others
- Age group vs gender
- Slicers for Month, Category and Channel

## 3. Order Tracking Dashboard

An interactive tracker showing each order's progress from dispatch to delivery.

- Three stages: Order Dispatch, On the way, Delivery Today
- Checkboxes update the status with one click
